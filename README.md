# FUTURE_DS_01
FUTURE_DS_01 — Online Retail Sales Analytics

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing online retail sales data to identify revenue trends, customer purchasing patterns, and top-performing products. The goal is to turn raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations that can help improve sales performance.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.

Identify top-performing products and sales trends.

Understand customer purchasing behavior.

Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.

Build an interactive Excel dashboard and a business report.

Provide data-driven recommendations for business growth.

📂 Dataset

The project uses an Online Retail transactional dataset containing invoice details, product codes, quantities, invoice dates, unit prices, and customer IDs.

The original dataset contains approximately 541,909 transaction records.

🛠️ Tools Used

Microsoft Excel — Dashboard, charts, and business reporting.

Python — Data cleaning and preprocessing.

CSV — Structured data storage and analysis.

📊 Key Business Findings

The analysis identified the following results:

Total Revenue: £10,642,110.80

Total Orders: 19,960

Unique Customers: 4,372

Unique Products: 4,000+

Units Sold: Approximately 11.6 million

These findings provide an overview of sales performance and help identify opportunities to improve revenue and customer engagement.

🧹 Data Cleaning

The data preparation process included:

Removing duplicate records.

Excluding cancelled transactions.

Removing records with non-positive quantities or unit prices.

Preparing sales data for analysis.

Retaining transaction records with missing customer IDs for transaction-level sales analysis.

💡 Business Recommendations

Focus on top-performing products: Maintain sufficient stock of products that contribute significantly to revenue.

Improve customer retention: Use customer purchase patterns to develop loyalty programs and targeted offers.

Optimize inventory: Use historical sales trends to support inventory planning and reduce stock-related issues.

Run targeted promotions: Design promotions around relevant products and purchasing patterns.

Monitor sales performance: Regularly review revenue, orders, and product performance through the dashboard.

🚀 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how retail transaction data can be cleaned, analyzed, and presented through a business-focused dashboard. The insights help summarize sales performance and support more informed decisions related to inventory, customer engagement, and revenue growth.
